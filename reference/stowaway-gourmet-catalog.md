# Stowaway Gourmet meals catalog (stowawaygourmet.com/collections/all-meals)

Scraped 2026-07-26 at Lawrence's direction. Single meals only — the Emergency
Supply Kit bundle and gift cards were skipped. Values are per-pouch, computed
verbatim from each product's back-label Nutrition Facts image (per-serving ×
servings per container; most labels are 2 servings/pouch). Net oz is the
Shopify variant shipping weight (grams ÷ 28.35), since the labels print
contents in grams only.

Exceptions:
- Andouille & Shrimp Jambalaya publishes no Nutrition Facts panel anywhere;
  kcal 633 / protein 30 g / 4.77 oz are the front-of-pouch printed values
  (seed uses the Shopify 4.51 oz shipping weight for consistency); carbs and
  fat stay null.
- Penne Alla Vodka's panel prints serving size 4.94 oz (125 g) while the pouch
  front says 4.41 oz (125 g) — grams agree, the oz is a label typo.
- Tiramisu Bites label says "approx 3 servings"; per-pouch = 3 × per-serving.
- Comrade Doeganoff's product page says "36 grams of protein"; its label reads
  21 g × 2 servings = 42 — the label was used.
