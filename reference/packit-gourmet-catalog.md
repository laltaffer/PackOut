# Packit Gourmet meals catalog (packitgourmet.com/trail-meals/view-all-meals/)

Scraped 2026-07-26 at Lawrence's direction. Single meals only — the Long
Weekend bundles, Best Sellers, and Happy Hour Snack Pack were skipped. Values
are per-pouch from each product's Nutrition Facts label image (per-serving ×
servings per container; most are 1 serving/pouch). Net oz is the product
page's "Meal Net Weight". Slot from the site's own categories: breakfasts →
breakfast, cool-water entrees → lunch, hot entrees → dinner, desserts and
Happy Hour extras → snack. Prep 'cook' where the product page requires
boiling water or a fry pan; cool-water meals are ready.

Multi-serving pouches (per-pouch = label × servings): Happy Hour dips are
2 servings; Moonshine Margarita is 4 servings (mix only, BYO tequila).

Exceptions:
- Ramen Rescues are BYO-noodle upgrade kits (broth, veg, meat) — 60 kcal is
  genuinely the whole kit, noodles not included; no net weight published.
- Tex-Mex Breakfast Tacos: no net weight on page or label.
- Cajun Ranch Chicken Salad: product page says 2.8 oz but the package front
  and label (92 g) both say 3.2 — the package was used.
- Diner Deluxe Eggs and Tex-Mex Tacos require a fry pan; marked cook.
